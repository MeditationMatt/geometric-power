The Theory of Geometric Power

A Framework for Reorganizing Human and Non-Human Authority Through Spatiotemporal First Principles

By Matthew William Ready
Port Townsend, Washington

Abstract

This work introduces the Theory of Geometric Power, a novel framework for understanding and reorganizing authority structures in human civilization. The theory posits that power operates across multiple dimensions, with geometric authority — the control of bounded space through time — constituting the foundational layer upon which all other forms of power are built.

Historical analysis reveals that the most persistent and violent human conflicts occur precisely at geometric boundary disputes, suggesting that stabilizing this primary dimension is prerequisite to lasting peace.

The Hierarchy of Power Dimensions

1. Geometric Authority

The map tracing the boundaries of power of different authorities. Anywhere you have disagreements about boundaries, you have a high likelihood of dangerous conflict. This is the first and most fundamental dimension — all others build upon it.

2. Hierarchical Authority

The map of human authority relationships as defined by codified social structures: Catholic Clergy, Military, Secret societies like Freemasons, Corporations. These are the visible, documented power structures.

3. Hidden Hierarchy

The actual order of influence humans hold in groups. In many groups, decisions appear to be made through voting or consensus, but in reality a few individuals hold secret influence. The decisions are actually made in back rooms, saunas, hot tubs, and long walks in the woods.

Key Insight: Power has many dimensions in human society, but the first dimension is always geometric. The first era of history in any civilization will always contain a period of intense fighting over geometric authority boundaries. After that is relatively stable, then the internal mechanisms of power can be developed.

The Principle of Immutable First Definition

For a geometric power system to work, everyone must agree on a single origin point in four-dimensional space-time (x, y, z, t). But how do we prevent endless fighting over where this point should be?

The solution: First Mover Advantage. The very first location suggested by any being — human, animal, AI, alien, saint, angel, demon, or god — becomes the permanent, unalterable reference point. No debates. No changes. Ever.

Like Bitcoin's 21 million coin limit, some variables are best set once. Otherwise, the clever ones will fight to the death to change them to their advantage. Best not to open the door.

The Structure: 12 Wedges

From the vortex point, lines extend in 12 directions (like a clock), creating 12 infinite wedges of space-time. Vertical planes extend up and down perpendicular to Earth's surface.

  • Southwest wedge = 1, going clockwise to 12
  • Level 13 = Everything below 100ft underground AND everything above 42,000ft altitude
  • The entire universe is divided into 12 wedges + Level 13 (inner/outer zones)

Universal Positioning

All celestial bodies, spaceships, and beings are positioned relative to this fixed point at the moment of definition. If you didn't exist at that moment, your position is determined by where your ancestors were.

Even if Earth is destroyed, the point in space-time never moves. This creates a permanent, cosmic frame of reference for all power relationships.

Key Benefits

  • Eliminates Foundational Disputes — By accepting the first-proposed origin point as permanent, the system prevents infinite regression of arguments.
  • Creates Universal Reference Frame — All beings can be positioned within the same coordinate system, enabling genuine multi-species diplomacy.
  • Exposes Hidden Power Structures — By mapping power geometrically, unofficial power brokers can no longer operate invisibly.
  • Provides Clean Foundation — A neutral starting point untainted by historical claims or accumulated corruption.
  • Decentralizes Authority Naturally — The 12-wedge system distributes space-time equally. No single wedge has inherent supremacy.
  • Includes Non-Human Intelligence — Explicitly accounts for extraterrestrial civilizations, AI entities, and other non-human intelligences by objective spatial coordinates.
  • Transcends Planetary Destruction — The coordinate system persists across cosmic timescales.
  • Democratizes Entry — Anyone could have proposed the first point. Once set, all participants have equal standing.
  • Reduces Boundary Violence — Clear, uncontestable coordinates eliminate the primary driver of territorial conflict.
  • Enables Verifiable Positioning — Any entity's position can be calculated and verified mathematically.

The Theory of Geometric Power represents a paradigm shift — from authority derived through conquest, inheritance, or accumulation to authority derived from verifiable position in space-time relative to an immutable first principle.

It offers humanity (and its potential allies) a path to reorganize civilization on foundations that are transparent, universal, and incorruptible.
